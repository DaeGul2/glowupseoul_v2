footage licensed under the **Mixkit Stock Video Free License** (commercial use
allowed, no attribution required) — https://mixkit.co/license/ — plus our own
motion graphics (chat UI, chips, typography). No AI-generated imagery.

4625 (airport), 4196 (hotel room), 52163, 52153, 22529, 52169, 24701, 52154,
51187 (skin care), 28147, 48385, 4766 (clinic), 9267 (tea).
Each clip's page showed `license/#videoFree` when downloaded (2026-10-05).
