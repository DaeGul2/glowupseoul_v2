All questions
Can I come alone?

Yes, many patients come on their own. Your coordinator can be with you at the consultation and treatment, and there when you wake up from surgery.

Is it really free? How do you make money?

It’s free for you. Partner clinics pay us a referral fee, and only after your treatment is done. You’d pay the clinic directly, at the same price as any walk-in patient.

How do you know a clinic is trustworthy?

Certifications, how many cases a clinic has done and government recognition are good places to start. Personally, I look for clinics where the doctors listen, explain things and genuinely want to solve your problem rather than treat you as a one-time customer. Those are the clinics I’d suggest to you.

How many days do I need in Seoul?

For most skin treatments, 1–2 days in Seoul is often enough. For surgery, around 7–10 days is a safer plan, and most surgeons advise not flying for 48–72 hours afterwards. Your doctor will confirm what applies to you.

It’s my first treatment in Korea. What would you suggest?

It really depends on your skin and what you’d like to change, so I’d love to hear your concern first. If you’d like a gentle start, many first-timers enjoy a skin booster. It suits most skin types and people often notice a glow within two to three weeks.
