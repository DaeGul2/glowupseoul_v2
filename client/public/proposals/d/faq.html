Getting started

What does a beauty concierge actually do?

I’m a bridge between you and clinics in Seoul. If you have doubts about pricing, or a treatment you want for a specific concern, I find the right doctors and clinics for you, then stay with you through it.

Is it really free? How do you make money?

Yes, it’s free for you. Partner clinics pay us a referral fee, and only after your treatment is completed. You pay the clinic directly, at the same price as any walk-in patient.

Do I need to know what treatment I want?

No. It helps to know your own standards, because almost every clinic has a salesperson and their advice can be misleading. If you don’t know yet, tell me your concern and we start from there.

How far in advance should I message you?

2–4 weeks before your trip is ideal for skin treatments, and earlier for surgery. Already in Seoul? Message anyway. Same-week appointments are often possible.

Clinics & safety

How do you know a clinic is trustworthy?

Officially: certifications, case volume and government recognition. Personally: I trust clinics that genuinely care. The doctors listen, communicate, and want to solve your problem instead of treating you as a one-time customer. Those are the clinics I take you to.

Treatments

First time in Korea. Where should I start?

Skin boosters. They work on almost every skin type, give that glossy glass-skin look, and you see results in two to three weeks.

Isn’t botox just for wrinkles?

Outside Korea, most people know botox for wrinkles. Here, “skin botox” is used as a skin booster for pore refinement and that glass-skin look. It’s a different treatment.

Botox or skin boosters?

Often both. A little botox if you want to prevent wrinkles, a skin booster to give your skin more nutrients. It depends on your concern.

Skin booster or laser?

Boosters target your skin overall. If you’re not sure what you need, a booster makes your skin feel better. Lasers target specific problems, so choose them after a consultation with a doctor.

Price

Why not book the cheapest clinic?

The cheapest price is usually a bait price. Once you’re there, other treatments get added and the total ends up similar or higher. Always compare the price of everything you’ll actually get.

▶ Watch Romie answer this
Are the prices on this site final?

No. They’re reference prices from partner clinics so you can plan. You get a full written quote before anything is booked.

How do I pay?

You pay the clinic directly, by card or cash, on the day. You never pay us.

Your trip

Gangnam or Apgujeong?

Gangnam has more skin clinics, Apgujeong has more plastic surgeons. Med-spa style treatments: Gangnam. Surgery: Apgujeong. I’ll point you to the right area for your goal.

▶ Watch Romie answer this
Can I come alone?

Yes, many patients do. Your coordinator is with you at consultation and treatment, and there when you wake up from surgery.

How many days do I need in Seoul?

1–2 days for most skin treatments. 7–10 days for surgery, and no flying for 48–72 hours after most procedures.

I don’t speak Korean. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Your coordinator interprets everything in real time, before, during and after your treatment. We speak English, 中文 and Bahasa.

Do I need a medical visa?

For most short skin treatments, a regular tourist entry is enough. For longer surgical stays, ask us and we’ll check what applies to your passport.

Aftercare

Is it normal to still have bumps after treatment?

It depends on your skin type, but it can take 1–2 weeks to settle. Send me a photo and I’ll check with the clinic if needed.

Do you still help after I fly home?

Yes. We check in on day 1, 7 and 30, and you can message any time. Even after you leave Korea, we’ll always care for you.
