Planning

How does it work?

Four simple steps, all on WhatsApp.

  1. Tell me your dates and what you’d like to change.
  2. I suggest treatments and 2–3 vetted clinics, with prices.
  3. I book everything for you.
  4. I meet you at the clinic and translate.
Ask Romie about this▶ Watch Romie answer
How early should I message you?2–4 weeks

2–4 weeks before your trip is ideal, as popular clinics fill up. I can often still find a slot within a few days. For surgery, allow at least 2 weeks.

Ask Romie about this
What should I send you first?Saves a day

Your dates in Seoul, what you’d like to improve and any treatments you’ve had before. Add 2–3 photos in daylight, no makeup: front and both sides.

Ask Romie about this
How many days do I need in Seoul?

For skin treatments, one day is often enough. Book it early in your trip, not the day you fly, so you can see the doctor again if needed. For surgery, plan 7–10 days.

Ask Romie about this
Do I need to decide my treatments first?

No. The doctor checks your skin first and you decide together. My suggestions help you choose the right clinic.

Ask Romie about this
Can I talk to the doctor before I fly?

Clinics don’t usually do video calls before booking. I send them your photos and questions and bring back their written advice.

Ask Romie about this
Can my friend, partner or mum come too?

Of course. I book you in together, often at the same time. Men are very welcome too.

Ask Romie about this
Can you help with anything besides clinics?

I focus on skin and aesthetic clinics, and I can also book health check-ups. Happy to share restaurant tips too.

Price & payment

Is your service free?Free

Yes. Partner clinics pay me a referral fee, the same at every clinic. You pay the clinic’s normal price, the same as walking in.

Ask Romie about this
How much will my treatments cost?

It depends on the clinic and the dose. I send quotes from 2–3 clinics before you book, so you can compare.

Ask Romie about this
Can I get a tax refund?VAT 10%

Not anymore. Korea’s tax refund on cosmetic treatments ended on 31 December 2025, so 10% VAT now applies. Most price lists exclude it, so I always flag it in your quote.

Ask Romie about this
How do I pay?Card OK

At the clinic on the day, by card or cash. There’s no extra charge for cards. If a clinic needs a booking deposit, I’ll tell you first.

Ask Romie about this
Is there a consultation fee?

Usually not, if you have your treatment the same day. Some clinics charge a small fee if a reserved consultation is cancelled.

Ask Romie about this
Are there discounts or promotions?

Many clinics run monthly event prices and first-visit offers. I check what’s on for your dates and add it to your quote.

Ask Romie about this
I’ve already picked a clinic. Can you still help?

Yes. I can book it and come with you to translate. If it isn’t one of my partner clinics, there’s a support fee and I’ll tell you the amount upfront.

Yes. I meet you at the clinic, translate the consultation and stay with you through the treatment.

Where do we meet? Can you pick me up?

We meet at the clinic. I send you the map pin and the Korean address for your taxi. Airport pickup comes with Premium Care.

Most partner clinics have English-speaking doctors or staff, and I translate either way. Chinese-speaking help is available too.

How long will I be at the clinic?1–2 hours

Usually 1–2 hours for one or two treatments, including the consultation and numbing. A full plan can take 3–4 hours.

Ask Romie about this
Will it hurt?

Most treatments feel like heat or a quick pinch. Numbing cream is standard, and many clinics offer laughing gas or sleep sedation for an extra fee. Tell me if you’re sensitive.

Ask Romie about this
Are clinics open at weekends?

Most are open on Saturdays and closed on Sundays. A few open on Sundays, and I plan around your days.

Treatments

It’s my first time. What should I try?

Start gentle. A skin booster like Rejuran or a no-downtime lifting device is a common first step. The doctor confirms what suits your skin.

Ask Romie about this▶ Watch Romie answer
When can I wear makeup or go sightseeing?

After lifting devices, usually straight away. Injections can leave small bumps or bruises for 1–2 days, and microneedling like Potenza some redness for 1–2 days.

Ask Romie about this
Can I do several treatments in one day?

Often, yes. Lifting devices and skin boosters are commonly done together. Your doctor decides what’s safe to combine.

Ask Romie about this
How long do results last?

Lifting devices: around 6–12 months. Botox: 3–4 months. Skin boosters build up over a series. Your doctor suggests how often.

Ask Romie about this
What should I avoid afterwards?

For a few days, skip alcohol, saunas and strong sun, and wear sunscreen. Your clinic gives you exact aftercare.

Ask Romie about this
I’m pregnant, on medication or have a skin condition. Is that OK?

Tell me before we book. Some treatments aren’t suitable, so I check with the doctor first.
