Quick answer

Most visitors come to Seoul for non-surgical skin treatments such as skin boosters, lifting devices, lasers and injectables. Many of these have little or no downtime, so 1–2 days in Seoul is often enough. It can help to compare clinics on credentials and how well doctors listen, to look at the total price rather than the cheapest headline price, and to message a coordinator 2–4 weeks before you travel.

Why people come to Seoul for skin treatments

Seoul has thousands of clinics, with more than three thousand in Gangnam alone. Visitors often tell us they come for modern equipment, experienced doctors and prices that can be more affordable than at home. With that much choice, the hard part is usually not finding a clinic but finding the right one for you.

Where in Seoul: Gangnam or Apgujeong?

As a general rule, Gangnam has more skin clinics and Apgujeong has more plastic surgeons. For skin treatments Gangnam is a good place to start, and for surgery you might look at Apgujeong. I’m happy to suggest an area once I know your goal. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

How long to stay

For most skin treatments, 1–2 days in Seoul is often enough. For surgery, around 7–10 days is a safer plan, and most surgeons advise not flying for 48–72 hours afterwards. Your doctor will confirm what applies to you.

When to start planning

If you can, messaging 2–4 weeks before your trip usually gives us enough time for skin treatments, and a little earlier for surgery. Already in Seoul? Feel free to message anyway and I’ll see what’s possible.

Before you fly

  • Send clear photos of your concernNatural light, no filters, front and side
  • List the skincare you use nowEspecially retinoids, acids or prescription creams
  • Note allergies or past reactions
  • Stop retinoids 5–7 days before laserWe’ll remind you when
  • Confirm your travel datesSo we can book around your schedule

What treatment day can look like

  1. Before: Message & consultation. We talk through your skin, match you with the right clinic and send a full written quote.
  2. Day 1: Treatment day. Your coordinator meets you, comes to the clinic with you, translates throughout and walks you to the pharmacy.
  3. Day 2: Check & skincare shopping. Some treatments need a next-day check. Then we point you to the best skincare shops in Seoul.
  4. +7 days: We check in. Wherever you are, we message to see how your skin is settling.
