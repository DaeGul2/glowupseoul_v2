Why the cheapest clinic in Seoul usually isn’t

The lowest price on the menu is often a bait price. Compare the total of everything you’ll get.

Romie · Glow Up Seoul · Updated 17 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

The cheapest treatment on a menu is often a bait price: once you’re in the clinic, other treatments get added and the total ends up similar or higher. Compare the total price of everything in your plan, and ask for a written quote before you book.
