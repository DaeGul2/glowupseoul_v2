Is the cheapest clinic in Seoul really the cheapest?

Why it can be worth looking past the lowest price on the menu and comparing the total for everything you’d get.

Romie · Updated 17 Oct 2026 · 3 min read
In this guide

  2. Comparing the whole plan

Quick answer

Sometimes the cheapest treatment on a menu is there to bring you in, and once other treatments are added the total can end up similar or higher. Comparing the total price of everything in your plan, and asking for a written quote before you book, usually gives a fairer picture.

Look past the headline price

It’s worth looking beyond the lowest price. Sometimes the cheapest option on a menu is there to bring you in, and once other treatments are added the total can end up similar or higher. Comparing the total for everything you’d actually get usually gives a fairer picture. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Comparing the whole plan

It can help to ask for the total price of everything in your plan, not just the headline treatment. That total is usually the fairest number to compare between clinics.

Reference prices vs your quote

They’re reference prices from partner clinics to help you plan. You’d get a full written quote before anything is booked.

How Glow Up Seoul is paid

It’s free for you. Partner clinics pay us a referral fee, and only after your treatment is done. You’d pay the clinic directly, at the same price as any walk-in patient.
