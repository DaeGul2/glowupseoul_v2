What Koreans actually get done at skin clinics (we asked on the street)

Regular Botox, skin boosters and Ulthera came up most when we asked locals in Gangnam.

Quick answer

The locals we interviewed in Gangnam mostly kept it simple: regular Botox, skin boosters and Ulthera came up most, along with laser hair removal and acne care. Their advice to visitors was to compare clinics carefully, read reviews and take your time.

For our Seoul Skin Talk series we asked people on the streets of Gangnam what they actually get done. Faces are blurred in the videos.

A woman in her 40s

She told us there are more than three thousand clinics in Gangnam. The only treatment she does regularly is Botox. Among her friends, Ulthera and skin boosters are the most popular. For daily care she credits thorough cleansing, yoga or pilates and eight hours of sleep, and says a healthy routine matters more than any treatment. Watch episode 1.

Korean men

A 33-year-old told us he used to get acne peels from time to time but doesn’t get anything done now. Two men in their 20s said they go to budget “factory” clinics for simple things like laser hair removal, and to more expensive clinics when the result depends on the doctor’s skill.

Their advice for visitors: there are a lot of clinics, so compare them carefully, read reviews and take your time choosing. Watch the episode.

Young women in Seoul

An 18-year-old told us she has had acne extraction, and that friends who are stressed about acne go to clinics. Asked what she would recommend to visitors: Ulthera, a skin booster for clearer skin, and scar treatment. Watch the episode.
