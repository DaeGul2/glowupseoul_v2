What it is

A picosecond laser that shatters pigment and refreshes tone with ultra-short pulses — gentler on surrounding skin than older lasers.

How it works

Pulses measured in trillionths of a second break pigment into fine dust your body clears away, while a focused mode refines pores and texture.

Often chosen for

Pores

Pico's ultra-short laser pulses gently stimulate collagen beneath the skin while refining the surface. This firms the skin around pores and smooths the surrounding texture so openings look tighter.

Rough texture

Picosecond energy creates micro-zones of controlled renewal with minimal heat damage, prompting the skin to rebuild smoother. It is an excellent low-downtime option for rough or uneven texture and shallow scarring.

Pigmentation

Pico's picosecond pulses shatter melanin into ultra-fine particles the body clears easily, making it a gold standard for stubborn pigment such as melasma, freckles and sun spots, with less risk of rebound darkening than older lasers.

Why people love it

  • Fades spots, freckles and dullness
  • Refines pores and overall tone

Before your appointment

  • Send Romie a clear photo of the area in natural light, so she can suggest clinics that fit.
  • Let your doctor know about any medication, supplements, allergies or past treatments.
  • If you use retinoids, you may be asked to pause them 5–7 days before a laser. Your clinic will confirm.
  • Plan your days so the downtime (no downtime) fits around your trip.

After your treatment

  • Mild redness that settles the same day.
  • Strict sun protection needed afterward
  • Follow the aftercare your clinic gives you, and message Romie if anything feels off. We check in on day 1, 7 and 30.
