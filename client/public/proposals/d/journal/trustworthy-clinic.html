How to tell if a clinic in Seoul is trustworthy

Some signals worth checking, and the one Romie looks for most: doctors who listen.

Quick answer

Official signals like certifications, case volume and government recognition are a good place to start. It also helps to look for doctors who listen and explain things, and to compare a few clinics rather than choosing one only because it’s everywhere online.

Signals worth checking

Certifications, how many cases a clinic has done and government recognition are good places to start. Personally, I look for clinics where the doctors listen, explain things and genuinely want to solve your problem rather than treat you as a one-time customer. Those are the clinics I’d suggest to you. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Visibility isn’t everything

You can, but I’d suggest comparing a few options too. A clinic that’s everywhere online usually has a big marketing budget. That doesn’t make it a bad clinic, but it might not be the best fit for what you need. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.
