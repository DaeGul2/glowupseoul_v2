How to tell if a clinic in Seoul is trustworthy

Some signals worth checking, and the one Romie looks for most: doctors who listen.

Romie · Glow Up Seoul · Updated 17 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

Official signals like certifications, case volume and government recognition are a good place to start. It also helps to look for doctors who listen and explain things, and to compare a few clinics rather than choosing one only because it’s everywhere online.

Signals worth checking

Certifications, how many cases a clinic has done and government recognition are good places to start. Personally, I look for clinics where the doctors listen, explain things and genuinely want to solve your problem rather than treat you as a one-time customer. Visibility isn’t everything
