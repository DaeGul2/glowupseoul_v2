How to tell if a clinic in Seoul is trustworthy

Official certificates first, then the thing that matters most: doctors who actually listen.

Romie · Glow Up Seoul · Updated 17 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

Check official signals first: certifications, case volume and government recognition. Then look for doctors who listen and explain. Don’t choose a clinic just because it is everywhere on social media.

Official signals, and the one I trust most

Officially: certifications, case volume and government recognition. Personally: I trust clinics that genuinely care. The doctors listen, communicate, and want to solve your problem instead of treating you as a one-time customer. Those are the clinics I take you to. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Don’t judge a clinic by how often you see it
