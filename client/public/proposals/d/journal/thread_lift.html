What it is

Dissolvable PDO threads placed under the skin give an immediate lift and keep working as they dissolve.

How it works

Fine barbed threads gently reposition soft tissue for an instant lift, then stimulate collagen along their path over the following months.

What to expect

You'll see a lift immediately, with some tightness as it settles. Best for those who want more than HIFU but less than surgery.

Good for

Sagging & firmness

Dissolvable threads are placed under the skin to physically reposition sagging tissue upward at once, anchoring it as the barbs grip. As the threads dissolve they also stimulate collagen along their path, so the lift is both immediate and self-reinforcing.

Why people love it

  • Immediate lift for mild–moderate sagging
  • Stimulates collagen as threads dissolve

Good to know

  • Done under local anesthesia

Recovery: Some tightness, swelling or bruising for a few days.
