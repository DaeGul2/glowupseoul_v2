Your first treatment in Korea: where to start

Start with a skin booster, know what you want before you walk in, and give yourself 1–2 days in Seoul.

Romie · Glow Up Seoul · Updated 17 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

Start with a skin booster: it suits almost every skin type and you see results in two to three weeks. Before you book, decide what you actually want, because most clinics have a salesperson. Plan 1–2 days in Seoul for skin treatments and message us 2–4 weeks ahead.

Start with a skin booster

Skin boosters. They work on almost every skin type, give that glossy glass-skin look, and you see results in two to three weeks. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Know what you want before you walk in

No. It helps to know your own standards, because almost every clinic has a salesperson and their advice can be misleading. If you don’t know yet, tell me your concern and we start from there. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Don’t copy someone else’s treatment list

Please don’t follow someone else’s journey religiously. Everyone reacts differently. Get a consultation and build your own plan. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

How many days you need

1–2 days for most skin treatments. 7–10 days for surgery, and no flying for 48–72 hours after most procedures.

When to message us

2–4 weeks before your trip is ideal for skin treatments, and earlier for surgery. Already in Seoul? Message anyway. Same-week appointments are often possible.

Before you fly: a quick checklist

  • Send clear photos of your concernNatural light, no filters, front and side
  • List the skincare you use nowEspecially retinoids, acids or prescription creams
  • Note allergies or past reactions
  • Stop retinoids 5–7 days before laserWe’ll remind you when
  • Confirm your travel datesSo we can book around your schedule
