Your first treatment in Korea: where to start

A few gentle suggestions for your first visit: what you might try, how to prepare and how long to stay.

Romie · Glow Up Seoul · Updated 17 Oct 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

It really depends on your skin and what you’d like to change. If you’d like a gentle start, many first-timers enjoy a skin booster. It can also help to have a rough idea of what you want before you visit, to plan 1–2 days in Seoul for skin treatments, and to message us 2–4 weeks ahead.

A gentle first step

It really depends on your skin and what you’d like to change, so I’d love to hear your concern first. If you’d like a gentle start, many first-timers enjoy a skin booster. It suits most skin types and people often notice a glow within two to three weeks. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Having a rough idea helps

Not at all. It can help to have a rough idea, since most clinics have someone whose job is to sell and their advice can pull you in different directions. If you’re not sure yet, just tell me your concern and we can figure it out together. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Your plan can be your own

I’d gently suggest not following someone else’s list too closely. Everyone’s skin reacts differently, so a consultation can help you build a plan that fits you. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

How long to stay

For most skin treatments, 1–2 days in Seoul is often enough. For surgery, around 7–10 days is a safer plan, and most surgeons advise not flying for 48–72 hours afterwards. Your doctor will confirm what applies to you.

When to message us

If you can, messaging 2–4 weeks before your trip usually gives us enough time for skin treatments, and a little earlier for surgery. Already in Seoul? Feel free to message anyway and I’ll see what’s possible.

Before you fly: a quick checklist

  • Send clear photos of your concernNatural light, no filters, front and side
  • List the skincare you use nowEspecially retinoids, acids or prescription creams
  • Note allergies or past reactions
  • Stop retinoids 5–7 days before laserWe’ll remind you when
  • Confirm your travel datesSo we can book around your schedule
