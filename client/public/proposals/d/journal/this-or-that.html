Quick answer

Botox and skin boosters do different jobs, so some people choose both. Skin boosters tend to work on your skin overall, while lasers usually target something specific. As a rough guide, Gangnam has more skin clinics and Apgujeong more plastic surgeons. Your doctor can help you decide.

Botox or skin booster?

They do different jobs, so some people end up choosing both. Botox is often used to soften or prevent wrinkles, while a skin booster gives your skin more nourishment. Your doctor can help you decide based on your concern. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Isn’t Botox just for wrinkles?

Not always. Outside Korea most people know botox for wrinkles, but here you’ll also hear about “skin botox”, which is used more like a skin booster for pores and a smoother, glassy look. It’s worth asking your doctor which kind they mean. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Skin booster or laser?

As a rough guide, skin boosters work on your skin overall, so they can be a nice choice if you’re not sure what you need. Lasers usually target something specific, so I’d suggest deciding on those after talking with a doctor. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.

Gangnam or Apgujeong?

As a general rule, Gangnam has more skin clinics and Apgujeong has more plastic surgeons. For skin treatments Gangnam is a good place to start, and for surgery you might look at Apgujeong. I’m happy to suggest an area once I know your goal. Watch Romie answer this on Instagram.
